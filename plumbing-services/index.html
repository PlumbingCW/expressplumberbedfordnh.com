Plumbing Services in Bedford, NH

.

Bedford Express Plumbing handles the full range of residential and light commercial plumbing in Bedford, NH. Emergency calls are answered 24/7, and same-day appointments are offered when a plumber is free.

Call (603) 815-9179.

Find the service you need

.

Each page below explains the problem, how we fix it and what affects the price. If you are not sure which fits, call and a dispatcher will point you to the right one.

The most common calls are drain cleaning, water heater repair and leak detection. Sewer problems are covered under sewer line repair, sewer line cleaning and camera inspection.

Planned work such as water heater installation, repiping and plumbing installation is scheduled ahead, and our dispatch line is open 24/7 to book it.

Water heaters: repair, replacement and installation

.

Water heaters are the call we get most after drains. A leaking tank or a unit that has gone cold is handled through water heater repair or, when the tank has failed, water heater replacement. Planned swaps, heat pump models and added capacity go through water heater installation.

Tankless units have their own page at tankless water heater service, covering installation, the yearly flush and the repairs that are specific to them. For heating systems, our boiler repair work covers the water side only, and we refer burner and combustion work to a heating technician.

Drains, sewers and backups

.

A clogged sink or tub is drain cleaning. A line that keeps clogging may need hydro jetting, and a main line that is blocked is sewer line cleaning. When the cause is a damaged pipe, a camera inspection shows the problem and sewer line repair fixes it.

These services are often linked. A camera inspection after a repeat clog tells you whether cleaning is enough, and cleaning before a camera pass clears the way so the picture is useful.

Water lines, leaks and pipes

.

Water that is where it should not be is covered by leak detection, burst pipe repair and water line repair and replacement. When pipes fail again and again, repiping replaces the whole system with new pipe.

Fixtures and appliances have their own pages. See toilet repair and installation, faucet and sink repair and garbage disposal repair and installation.

Basements, businesses and new work

.

Wet basements are handled through sump pump installation and repair and basement waterproofing. Offices, shops and apartment buildings are served under commercial plumbing, and remodels and additions under plumbing installation.

For larger projects, permits and inspections are set by the town. Ask the Bedford building department what applies to your work, and we will tell you what we find that could affect it.
