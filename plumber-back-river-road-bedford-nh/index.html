Where Back River Road is

.

Back River Road is in the eastern part of Bedford, and local guides describe connections toward Manchester and South River Road. Route 101 east of NH Route 114 is a freeway that runs to the Manchester border.

Because this side of town is close to the city line, we ask for your full street address and town. That keeps a call from getting routed to the wrong place.

Near the city line: Bedford or Manchester?

.

Addresses near a town line can be confusing. Your tax bill and your mailing address show which town you are in, and that decides which town office handles permits and records.

We work in both Manchester and Bedford. Tell us your town and we will point you to the right office for permit questions.

Where homes and businesses meet

.

Some properties here sit next to commercial uses, and the plumbing needs of a house and a shop are not the same. A house on a residential line needs a different approach than a storefront with a grease trap.

For business buildings, see commercial plumbing. For homes, the services above apply.

Before the plumber arrives

.

A few minutes of preparation can shorten the visit and reduce damage.

  • Find the main water shutoff and make sure you can reach it.
  • Clear the area under the sink or around the water heater.
  • Note when the problem began and what you have already tried.
  • Take photos of any water damage for your insurer.

Standards and references

.

Plumbing work follows published model codes such as the International Plumbing Code (IPC) from the International Code Council and the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) from IAPMO. The edition a town enforces is its own decision, so ask the Bedford building department which one applies to your project.
