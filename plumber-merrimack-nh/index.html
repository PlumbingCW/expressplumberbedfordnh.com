Private wells, PFAS and what to do about them

.

Merrimack is the town where the Saint-Gobain plant is located, and PFOA and PFAS contamination associated with it affected private wells in Merrimack and neighboring towns including Bedford. ATSDR recommends that private well owners monitor or treat their water and have it tested.

NHDES recommends that all private well owners test every three to five years, and says radon and arsenic are the most common contaminants. Use an accredited lab, and ask what the report means before you buy a filter. Plumbers can install treatment systems, but the test result should decide what you buy.

Before we arrive in Merrimack: what to do right now

.

Before a plumber arrives in Merrimack, take these steps, and check first whether you are on a well or on a water district line.

Find the main shutoff.

.

On public water it is near the meter. On a well it is often on the line near the pressure tank.

Shut off the well pump.

.

If you are on a well and water is escaping, turn off power to the pump at the breaker.

Contain the water.

.

Use towels or a wet vac and lift belongings off the floor.

Stop sewer or septic use.

.

If waste is backing up, do not flush or run laundry.

Photograph everything.

.

Capture the cause and the damage for your insurer.
