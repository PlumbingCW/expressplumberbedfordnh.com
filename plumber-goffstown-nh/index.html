When Goffstown calls us at night

.

River towns and hill towns have different emergencies, and Goffstown has both.

  • A sump pump that stops during spring melt or a heavy storm.
  • A basement taking on water at the wall or slab joint.
  • A frozen pipe in an exterior wall or an unheated space.
  • A drain that backs up into a bathroom or laundry area.
  • A water heater that has failed and is leaking.

Describe what you see and give the dispatcher your street. Same-day appointments are offered when a plumber is free, and a flooding basement is placed first at any hour.

Rivers, hills and wet basements

.

The Piscataquog River flows through the town, and Glen Lake and the slopes below the Uncanoonuc Mountains add to the runoff that reaches foundations. In a wet spring, the ground around a house can stay saturated for weeks.

That makes the sump pump the most important appliance in many Goffstown basements. Our sump pump service covers failed pumps and new systems, and basement waterproofing is the planned follow-up when a basement stays wet.

Village water precinct or private well

.

The Goffstown Village Water Precinct, established in 1891, serves the village section of town, and homes beyond its lines are generally on private wells. Which one you have determines who maintains the line and which problems you should expect.

Ask the precinct whether your address is on its system. If you are on a well, NHDES recommends testing every three to five years and says radon and arsenic are the most common contaminants.

Is emergency service available in Goffstown?

Yes. The line is staffed 24/7, and a plumber is dispatched as soon as one is free. Flooding and burst pipes are placed first.

Is my basement flooding because of the Piscataquog River?

River level and groundwater both play a part, and sometimes the cause is a failed pump or a clogged gutter. A plumber can check the pump and the discharge line first.

How do I know if I am on village water?

Ask the Goffstown Village Water Precinct whether your address is on its system. Homes outside its lines are generally on private wells.

Do you install battery backups for sump pumps?

Yes. A backup keeps the pump running during outages, which are common during storms.

Standards and references

.

Plumbing work follows published model codes such as the International Plumbing Code (IPC) from the International Code Council and the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) from IAPMO. For private wells, the New Hampshire Department of Environmental Services recommends testing every three to five years.

Before we arrive in Goffstown: what to do right now

.

Basement water and frozen pipes are the common Goffstown emergencies. These steps help while a plumber travels to you.

Check the pump.

.

Make sure the sump pump is plugged in and the breaker has not tripped.

Close the main shutoff.

.

If a pipe has burst, shut the main water supply.

Shut off the well pump.

.

If you are on a well, cut the pump breaker when water is escaping.

Stay clear of wet electrical.

.

Do not stand in water near outlets or the panel.

Lift and photograph.

.

Raise valuables off the floor and take photos for your insurer.
