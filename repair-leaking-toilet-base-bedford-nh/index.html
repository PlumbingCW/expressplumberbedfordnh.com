How to Repair a Leaking Toilet Base: What Bedford, NH Homes Need to Know

.

Water on the floor around a toilet is not always a leak from the toilet. It can be condensation, a loose supply line or a failed seal under the bowl, and it takes a short check to tell which.

This guide walks through the causes, the repair and the signs that the floor has been damaged.

Start by finding the source

.

Dry the floor and the outside of the toilet completely. Then check each possible source, one at a time.

Source.How to check.Fix.
Condensation on the tank.Tank feels cold and wet outside.Insulate or add a mixing valve.
Supply line or shutoff.Dry the connection and look for drips.Tighten or replace the line.
Tank bolts.Water beneath the tank.Tighten bolts or replace washers.
Base seal.Water appears at the base after flushing.Reset with a new wax ring.
Cracked bowl.Hairline crack and a slow drip.Replace the toilet.

Signs the wax seal has failed

.

The wax ring seals the base of the toilet to the drain flange. When it fails, water seeps out of the base after flushes.

  • A toilet that rocks even slightly.
  • Water at the base, mostly after flushing.
  • A sewage smell in the bathroom.
  • Stained or soft flooring near the toilet.
  • A toilet that was moved or reset recently.

A loose toilet breaks the seal over time, so a wobble is worth fixing even if no water shows yet.

Resetting the toilet with a new wax ring

.

The repair is a full reset, and it is similar to an installation.

  1. Close the shutoff, flush and sponge out the tank and bowl.
  2. Disconnect the supply line and remove the nuts on the closet bolts.
  3. Lift the toilet straight up and set it on a towel.
  4. Scrape off the old wax and check the flange for cracks.
  5. Install a new wax ring or seal, then lower the toilet straight down.
  6. Tighten the nuts evenly, reconnect the supply and test by flushing several times.

If the flange is cracked or below the floor surface, it needs a repair ring or a plumber, as a new ring cannot seal on a bad flange.

Checking the floor

.

After you lift the toilet, press on the floor around the flange with a screwdriver. If it is soft, spongy or dark, water has been getting in for some time.

A damaged subfloor needs repair before the toilet goes back, and the cause may be a long-term leak. A leak detection visit can confirm that no other pipe is feeding the damage.

When the toilet itself is the problem

.

A cracked bowl or tank cannot be repaired, and a toilet that clogs often or runs constantly may be worth replacing. Our toilet repair and installation service handles both.

If a bathroom is on the second floor and the ceiling below is stained, shut off the toilet's supply and call at once, because water may be spreading.

What to do before you start the repair

.

Close the shutoff behind the toilet and do not flush again. Do not use the toilet while the seal is off, and keep children and pets out of the bathroom while the bowl is lifted.

If the water on the floor is dark or smells of sewage, wear gloves, keep the area ventilated and call a plumber instead of cleaning it up yourself. Take photos of any damage first, in case you need them for an insurance claim.

Preventing the next leak

.

Most base leaks come back because the cause was never addressed. A few habits and checks keep a freshly set toilet sealed.

  • Check for rocking every few months, and tighten the closet bolts evenly if the bowl moves.
  • Do not stand on or lean hard against the toilet, which flexes the seal.
  • Replace tank-to-bowl bolts and washers when you reset the toilet, since old ones leak.
  • If you replace the flooring, set the flange at the new floor height before the toilet goes back.

A quick yearly inspection takes a minute. Look under the tank, around the supply line and at the base, and wipe each with a dry paper towel to reveal a slow drip.

Common questions

.
Is a leaking toilet base an emergency?

Not usually, unless water is spreading to a ceiling below or the toilet is overflowing. Shut off the supply and plan the repair soon.

How do I know if the leak is condensation?

Condensation appears on the outside of the tank on humid days and the water is clean and cold. A leak at the base appears after flushing.

Do I need to replace the toilet to fix the leak?

Not if the bowl is intact. A new seal and a level reset usually fix it.

Can a leaking toilet damage the floor?

Yes. Water that seeps into the subfloor can rot wood and cause mold. Check by pressing on the floor around the toilet.

Why does a toilet in an older Bedford house keep leaking after a reset?

The flange may sit below the finished floor after a remodel, which prevents a good seal. A spacer or a new flange fixes it.

.
