How to Install Kitchen Sink Plumbing in Bedford, NH

.

A kitchen sink looks simple until you are lying in the cabinet with a flashlight. The drain, the trap, two supply lines and often a dishwasher and disposal all meet in a few square feet, and each connection has to seal.

This guide covers how the parts fit and what to check, and it explains when moving a drain makes the job a plumber's.

The parts and how they connect

.

Under the sink, five groups of parts do the work.

  • Supply lines run from the shutoff valves to the faucet.
  • A strainer basket and tailpiece drain each basin.
  • A continuous waste tee joins a double basin to one trap.
  • A P-trap holds water to block sewer gas.
  • A trap arm carries waste into the wall drain.

A dishwasher drain hose and a garbage disposal join the same system, usually at a branch tailpiece or the disposal inlet.

Setting the sink and faucet

.

Mount the faucet on the sink before you set the sink, because there is far more room to work on a countertop than inside the cabinet. Connect the supply lines to the faucet at the same time.

Run a bead of plumber's putty under each strainer flange, press it into the basin and tighten the nut underneath. Wipe off the squeeze-out, set the sink in place and secure it according to the manufacturer's instructions.

Building the drain

.

Assemble the drain from the strainer down, and keep the slope toward the wall.

  1. Attach the tailpieces to the strainers and join them with the continuous waste tee.
  2. Add the trap so the horizontal run slopes slightly down toward the wall.
  3. Connect the trap arm to the wall stub with a slip joint and washer.
  4. Hand-tighten the nuts, then give a gentle extra turn with pliers.
  5. Run water in both basins and watch every joint for drips.

The trap arm must not run too far from the vent, or the trap can siphon dry. That limit depends on pipe size and the code the town enforces, so ask the Bedford building department if you are moving the drain.

Dishwasher and disposal connections

.

A dishwasher drains through a hose that loops high under the counter, or through an air gap, before it joins the sink drain or the disposal. The loop keeps dirty water from flowing back into the dishwasher.

If you are adding a disposal, see garbage disposal installation for the electrical and mounting steps, and knock out the dishwasher port inside the disposal before connecting the hose, since forgetting that step blocks the dishwasher drain.

Common mistakes to avoid

.

A few errors cause most leaks and slow drains.

Mistake.Result.Fix.
Trap installed backward or too low.Slow drain or odor.Reset with correct slope.
Over-tightened plastic nuts.Cracks and leaks.Replace and hand-tighten.
No tailpiece washer or the wrong washer.Drips at the joint.Use the right beveled washer.
Putty omitted at the strainer.Leak into the cabinet.Remove and reseat with putty.
Supply lines too tight or kinked.Failed line or low flow.Replace with correct length.

When to call a plumber

.

Replacing a sink in the same spot is a reasonable DIY project. Moving the sink, adding a second basin on a new wall or opening a wall for a new drain is not.

A plumber is also the right call if the old shutoff valves will not close, if you find corroded pipe in the wall or if the sink drains slowly even with new parts. In that case the line behind the wall may be partly clogged, and drain cleaning comes before more parts.

Bedford note: water quality at your faucet

.

Manchester Water Works, whose service area includes portions of Bedford, reported total hardness of 19.35 mg/L and a pH of 7.8 for 2018, with a range of 17.4 to 22.1 mg/L.

On that supply, faucet aerators and cartridges scale slowly. If you use a private well instead, hardness and iron can be very different, and NHDES recommends testing every three to five years.

Source type: published utility water-quality report and state agency well guidance.

Common questions

.
Do I need a new trap when I replace a sink?

Not always, but trap parts are inexpensive and old ones deform. Replacing the trap and washers is a good idea while the cabinet is empty.

Why does my new sink drain slowly?

Check the trap slope, the tailpiece length and whether the line behind the wall is clogged. A partly blocked line is the most common reason.

Can I install a kitchen sink without a plumber?

A same-place swap is a reasonable DIY job. Moving the drain, adding a basin or working with old pipe is better left to a plumber.

How do I stop the cabinet smelling after I reinstall the sink?

Check that the trap holds water and every joint is sealed. A dry trap or a loose slip joint lets sewer gas into the cabinet.

Which Bedford water source feeds my faucet?

Parts of Bedford are on Manchester Water Works, while others use private wells. Ask the town or test your tap to know what runs through your faucet.

Standards and references

.

Plumbing work follows published model codes such as the International Plumbing Code (IPC) from the International Code Council and the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) from IAPMO. The edition a town enforces is its own decision, so ask the Bedford building department which one applies to your project. For private wells, the New Hampshire Department of Environmental Services recommends testing every three to five years. The EPA WaterSense label identifies water-efficient toilets and faucets.
