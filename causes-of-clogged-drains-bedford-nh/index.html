Causes of Clogged Drains for Bedford, NH Homes

.

A clogged drain is rarely a mystery once you know where to look. The same few causes account for most blockages, and each leaves its own clues.

This guide explains what blocks drains in houses like those in Bedford, and which habits stop the problem from returning.

Kitchen drain causes

.

Kitchens clog because of what goes in them. Grease is the main culprit.

  • Cooking grease cools in the pipe and coats the walls.
  • Food scraps catch on the grease and build a plug.
  • Coffee grounds settle in low spots and pack together.
  • Starchy foods like rice and pasta swell with water.
  • Eggshells and fibrous scraps add bulk that does not flush away.

A garbage disposal does not make these safe. It grinds food finer, but the paste still settles in the line. See garbage disposal repair if yours is jamming.

Bathroom drain causes

.

Bathrooms clog differently.

Fixture.Common cause.Clue.
Sink.Hair, soap scum and toothpaste.Slow drain, gurgle.
Tub or shower.Hair and soap film.Standing water at the drain.
Toilet.Too much paper or items that should not be flushed.Rising water, weak flush.
Floor drain.Debris and sediment.Slow or smelly drain.

A hair catcher in tubs and showers prevents most bathroom clogs, and costs very little.

Main line causes

.

When more than one drain is slow, the problem is farther down the line.

  • Tree roots that have entered a joint or crack.
  • Wipes, even those labeled flushable, which do not break down.
  • A belly, which is a low spot that holds water and waste.
  • A collapsed or offset section of old pipe.
  • A full septic tank, if the house is on septic.

These need more than a plunger. Sewer line cleaning and a camera inspection find and clear them.

What does not work well

.

Quick fixes can make a clog worse.

  • Chemical drain openers can sit in a blocked pipe and damage old fittings.
  • A plunger on a clogged main line can push waste into other fixtures.
  • A hanger or wire can scratch pipe and snag.
  • Ignoring a slow drain lets a partial clog become a full one.

A hand auger helps with a single sink or tub. For repeat clogs, drain cleaning with a machine is more effective.

Habits that prevent clogs

.

Small changes cut down on calls.

  1. Wipe pans before washing and put grease in a container, not down the sink.
  2. Use strainers in kitchen sinks and hair catchers in showers.
  3. Flush only toilet paper, nothing else.
  4. Run hot water for a minute after washing dishes.
  5. Have a camera look at an old main line before problems arrive.

Reading the clues: where the clog probably is

.

How the drain behaves tells you how far along the line the problem sits. A single slow sink points to that fixture's trap or branch, while a toilet that gurgles when the tub drains points to a shared line.

What you notice.Likely location.First step.
One sink slow, others fine.That fixture's trap or branch.Clear the trap, then auger.
Tub backs up when the washer drains.The shared bathroom or laundry branch.Machine the branch.
Lowest drain overflows when upstairs fixtures run.The main line.Stop using water, call for main line cleaning.
Gurgling and a sewage smell.A blocked vent or main line.Call a plumber.

Seasonal causes in New Hampshire

.

Winter and spring bring their own clogs. In cold months, grease hardens faster in cool pipes along exterior walls, and in spring, saturated ground pushes roots into joints that were dry all winter.

If your drains slow right after the thaw, treat that as a sign to look at the main line rather than just the fixture, since groundwater and root growth both pick up as the ground warms.

Common questions

.
Why does my drain clog again right after cleaning?

Usually because the pipe wall is rough or damaged. A camera shows whether the pipe needs repair.

Are flushable wipes safe?

No. They do not break down like paper and are a common cause of main line clogs.

Can tree roots clog a drain?

Yes, they enter through joints and cracks and form a mat that catches waste.

How do I know if the clog is in the main line?

If several fixtures drain slowly, or the lowest drain backs up when you run water, the main line is the likely cause.

Why do older Bedford homes have more clogs?

Pipe installed decades ago can be rough, offset or rooted. A camera inspection tells you the state of your line.

Standards and references

.
