Tell the dispatcher your road and the problem. Same-day appointments are offered when a plumber is free, and nights and weekends are covered.

Long driveways and long water lines

.

On a lot set back from the road, the water line can run a long way underground before it reaches the house. A leak in that run may show up only as a wet patch in the yard or a higher bill, and the distance makes finding it harder.

Our leak detection work traces the line, and water line repair and replacement fixes it. Frost depth matters in New Hampshire, because a shallow line freezes.

Amherst Village District water, Pennichuck and wells

.

Pennichuck Water Works serves the Amherst Village District. Amherst had PFOA concerns in 2016, and in 2022 a PFAS-related main extension covered Cobbler Lane, Thatcher Drive and part of Amherst Street.

Homes outside the district rely on private wells. NHDES recommends testing every three to five years, and ATSDR recommends that well owners in affected areas monitor or treat their water. Ask Pennichuck whether your street is on its system.

Standards and references

.

Plumbing work follows published model codes such as the International Plumbing Code (IPC) from the International Code Council and the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) from IAPMO. The edition a town enforces is its own decision, so ask the Bedford building department which one applies to your project. For private wells, the New Hampshire Department of Environmental Services recommends testing every three to five years. The federal Agency for Toxic Substances and Disease Registry publishes guidance for private well owners in areas affected by PFAS.
