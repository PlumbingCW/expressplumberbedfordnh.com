Tankless Water Heater and Boiler in Bedford, NH

.

Many New Hampshire homes heat with a hydronic boiler, and when the water heater fails, the question comes up whether one system can serve both jobs. The answer depends on the boiler, the household and what you want to spend.

This guide compares the main options so you can ask the right questions before you buy.

Three ways to pair hot water and a boiler

.

There are three common arrangements, and each fits a different house.

Option.How it works.Good fit.
Separate tankless heater.A standalone gas or electric unit makes hot water. The boiler only heats the house.A good boiler and a failing tank.
Indirect tank on the boiler.The boiler heats water in a separate insulated tank.A boiler with enough capacity and a large household.
Combi boiler.One appliance supplies heat and hot water on demand.A full system replacement.

Separate tankless heater

.

A standalone tankless unit leaves your heating system alone. It needs its own gas line or heavy electric circuit, and its own vent. The boiler continues to heat the house on its own schedule.

This is the easiest change when the boiler is sound. See our tankless water heater service for installation and maintenance.

Indirect tank and combi boiler

.

An indirect tank is a storage tank with a coil inside that the boiler heats. It recovers quickly and lasts long, since it has no burner of its own, but it relies on the boiler running.

A combi boiler combines both functions in one appliance. It saves space and removes the separate heater, but a failure of the unit takes out heat and hot water together. The boiler, the venting and the combustion work belong to a licensed heating technician, and we refer that side of the job.

Questions to ask before choosing

.

A short list of questions saves money and regrets.

  1. How old is the boiler, and is it worth tying hot water to it?
  2. How many bathrooms and how much simultaneous use?
  3. Is there a gas line large enough for a tankless unit?
  4. Where will the vent run, and does the wall or roof allow it?
  5. What happens to hot water if the boiler fails in January?

Winter performance and what to expect

.

A tankless heater's output falls as the incoming water gets colder. In a New Hampshire January, the same unit that delivers plenty in July may struggle to keep two showers hot at once.

Ask the maker for the flow rate at the temperature rise you actually need, not at the best-case rise printed on the box. Sizing for the coldest month is what keeps the household happy in all twelve.

A boiler-fed indirect tank avoids this problem, because the water it stores is already warm. That is a real advantage in a house with heavy winter demand.

Common questions

.
Can a tankless heater run off my boiler?

Some systems do this with a heat exchanger, but most homes use a separate tankless unit or an indirect tank. A heating technician should size the boiler side.

Is a combi boiler right for a large family?

Large households can overload a combi, so size matters. An indirect tank often serves larger demand more steadily.

Do I need a permit?

Many projects need one. Ask the Bedford building department what applies to your project.

What if the boiler fails?

With a combi or indirect tank, hot water goes down with the boiler. A separate heater keeps hot water running.

Which Bedford office handles permits?

Contact the Bedford town office at 24 North Amherst Road and ask for the building department, since permits appear under Licenses and Permits on the town website.
