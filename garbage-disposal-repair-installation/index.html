Disposal emergencies: what to do before we arrive

.

A stuck disposal is annoying. One that floods the cabinet or backs up through the sink is a different matter.

  • Water leaks from the bottom or sides of the disposal.
  • The sink backs up and will not drain after you run the disposal.
  • The unit hums but will not turn, and the breaker trips.
  • A burning smell or smoke comes from the motor.

Tell the dispatcher what you see. We offer same-day visits when a plumber is free and answer at night and on weekends.

  1. Switch the disposal off at the wall switch and, if it smells hot, at the breaker.
  2. Never put a hand into the grinding chamber.
  3. Close the shutoff valves under the sink to stop water to the faucet.
  4. Place a bucket under any leak and move cabinet contents out.

Why disposals jam and leak

.

Most jams come from something hard or fibrous in the chamber, such as bone, fruit pits, produce stickers or a bottle cap. Others come from the unit being used with too little water, which lets food paste settle in the line.

Repair or replace

.

A disposal usually lasts eight to fifteen years. A jam, a reset or a loose connection is worth repairing. A rust-streaked housing, a leak from the body or a motor that has burned out is a replacement.

Horsepower and build vary. A half-horsepower unit handles a light household, and a larger, quieter model suits heavy use. We match the new unit's mounting to your sink flange where possible, so the installation is clean and quick.

What not to put in a disposal

.

A few habits protect the unit and the drain.

  • Grease, oils and fats, which coat the line and harden.
  • Coffee grounds in large amounts, which pack into sludge.
  • Pasta, rice and potato peels, which swell into paste.
  • Bones, fruit pits and shells.
  • Fibrous stalks like celery, corn husks and artichoke.

Run cold water before, during and after use. If the line behind the disposal keeps clogging, drain cleaning or hydro jetting clears the buildup.

Common questions

.
Do you handle garbage disposal emergencies at night or on weekends in Bedford?

Yes. The line is open 24/7. A leaking or flooding disposal is placed ahead of routine calls, and same-day service is offered when a plumber is free.

Is it safe to reach in and clear a jam?

No. Turn off the power and use a hex key or wrench from the bottom, or tongs from the top. Never put your hand in the chamber.

Why does my disposal smell?

Food residue builds on the chamber and the baffle. Running cold water and grinding ice or citrus can help, but a persistent smell may mean a clogged line.

Can you install a disposal where there was none?

Yes, if there is an outlet or a place for a switched outlet. We also reconnect the dishwasher drain.

How long does installation take?

Replacing like for like usually takes an hour or two, longer when new wiring is needed.
