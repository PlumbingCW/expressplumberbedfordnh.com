Emergency Faucet and Sink Repair in Bedford, NH

A faucet that will not shut off or a sink that is leaking under the cabinet needs attention before it floods the floor. Our 24/7 line takes the call at any hour, and while you wait you should close the shutoff valves under the sink and set a bucket or towels where water is falling.

When a faucet or sink leak is an emergency

.

A drip is a nuisance. A steady leak under a cabinet is not, because the cabinet floor hides the water until it has done damage.

  • Water pools in the cabinet under a sink or on the floor next to it.
  • A faucet will not shut off or sprays from the base.
  • A supply line connection is dripping steadily.
  • A shutoff valve is stuck, leaking or will not turn.

Describe the leak to the dispatcher and ask for the same-day slot. We offer same-day visits when a plumber is free and answer at night and on weekends.

  1. Turn the small shutoff valves under the sink clockwise to close them.
  2. If they will not close, shut the main water supply.
  3. Empty the cabinet so you can see where the water is coming from.
  4. Dry the cabinet floor and place a bucket under the leak.

What fails in faucets and sinks

.

Most faucet problems come from a few parts. Knowing which one is failing explains the repair and the cost.

Problem.Usual part.Repair.
Drip from the spout.Cartridge, washer or ceramic disc.Replace the cartridge or seat.
Leak at the handle.O-ring or packing.Replace the seals.
Leak under the sink.Supply line, drain slip nut or shutoff valve.Replace the line or fitting.
Weak flow.Clogged aerator or cartridge debris.Clean or replace parts.
Loose sink or faucet.Mounting hardware.Retighten or replace.
Shower temperature swing.Pressure-balancing valve cartridge.Replace the cartridge.

Shutoff valves and supply lines

.

The small valves under a sink are meant to let you stop water to one fixture. Many are old and have not been turned in years, and they often fail the first time they are asked to work. A valve that will not close, or that leaks when turned, is replaced as part of the repair.

Braided supply lines last a decade or two. The ones that burst usually do so at the crimped ends, and a burst line under a kitchen sink can pour out a lot of water in minutes. If yours are old, replace them while the cabinet is empty.

Kitchen, bathroom and shower fixes

.

Kitchen faucets add pull-down sprayers and filters that clog. Bathroom sinks add pop-up drains whose seals fail. Showers add valves behind the wall, where a failing cartridge can send hot or cold water into a drip that never fully stops.

If you are replacing a faucet, measure the number of holes in the sink and the spacing before you buy. If the sink itself is cracked or the countertop is being replaced, plumbing installation covers new fixtures and rough-in changes.

Hard water, soft water and wells

.

Mineral scale shortens the life of cartridges and aerators. Manchester Water Works reports total hardness of 19.35 mg/L for its 2018 supply, which is soft, so scale there is mild. Private wells vary widely.

If you rely on a well, an accredited lab test shows hardness, iron and other minerals, and NHDES recommends testing every three to five years. If your faucets clog or stain quickly, that test is the best next step.

Common questions

.
Do you handle faucet and sink emergencies at night or on weekends in Bedford?

Yes. The line is answered 24/7. A leak you cannot stop is placed ahead of routine calls, and same-day visits are offered when a plumber is free.

Is a dripping faucet worth fixing?

Yes. A drip of one drop per second wastes thousands of gallons a year and often points to a part that will fail completely.

Can you fix a faucet that is no longer made?

Often, by sourcing a compatible cartridge. If parts are unavailable, we help you choose a replacement.

Why will my shutoff valves not close?

Mineral buildup and age seize the stem. We replace the valve so you have working isolation next time.

Can you replace a kitchen sink?

Yes. We disconnect the drain and supply lines, set the new sink and reconnect everything. Countertop work is handled by your cabinet or countertop contractor.

Do you work in other towns?

Yes. We serve Manchester, Merrimack, Goffstown, Amherst and Hooksett.

Standards and references

.

Plumbing work follows published model codes such as the International Plumbing Code (IPC) from the International Code Council and the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) from IAPMO. The edition a town enforces is its own decision, so ask the Bedford building department which one applies to your project. For private wells, the New Hampshire Department of Environmental Services recommends testing every three to five years. The EPA WaterSense label identifies water-efficient toilets and faucets.
