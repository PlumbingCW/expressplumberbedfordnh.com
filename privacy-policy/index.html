Privacy Policy

.

Last updated 2026-11-14. This policy explains what information we collect when you call, email or visit this website.

Information we collect

.

When you call, we collect the information you give us, such as your name, address, phone number and a description of the problem. When you email, we collect your email address and the content of your message.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

Our website does not contain forms. Standard server logs may record your IP address, browser type and the pages visited.

How we use information

.

We use your information to schedule and complete service, to answer your questions, to send invoices and records and to improve our service. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

.

We share information only with people who help us serve you, such as a plumber assigned to your job, or when required by law.

Cookies and third parties

.

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts and embeds Google Maps. Those services may collect technical information under their own policies.

Your choices and contact

.

To ask about or correct your information, email contact@expressplumberbedfordnh.com or call (603) 815-9179. Our office is at 2 Executive Park Drive, Bedford, NH 03110.
