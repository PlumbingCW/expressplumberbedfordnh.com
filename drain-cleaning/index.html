Emergency Drain Cleaning in Bedford, NH

.

A drain that has stopped completely is an emergency once it backs up into a tub, a basement floor drain or the only kitchen you have. Our line is staffed 24/7, so call now, and while you wait stop running water, skip the toilet flushes and move anything off the floor near the lowest drain.

Drain cleaning is the removal of a blockage or buildup from a drain line, using a cable machine, a hydro jet or a hand tool matched to what is actually clogging the pipe. In Bedford, NH we clear kitchen lines, bathroom branches and the main drain that carries everything out of the house.

When a clogged drain is an emergency

.

Most single-sink slowdowns can wait for the next available appointment. Some situations cannot, and it helps to know which one you are in before you call.

  • Water or sewage rises from a floor drain, tub or shower when you flush a toilet or run the washer.
  • Every fixture in the house drains slowly at once, which points to the main line instead of one branch.
  • Toilets gurgle or bubble when a sink or shower drains.
  • A sewage smell comes with damp flooring near a drain.

If any of those match, tell the dispatcher in your first sentence. Same-day appointments are offered whenever a plumber is free, and after-hours calls are answered at night and on weekends just like daytime ones.

While you wait for the plumber:

  1. Stop using sinks, tubs, showers, the dishwasher and the washing machine.
  2. Do not run the garbage disposal into a blocked line.
  3. Skip chemical drain openers. They can sit in the pipe and splash on whoever opens it next.
  4. Keep children and pets out of any standing water and lift stored boxes off the floor.

Why drains clog in Bedford homes

.

Bedford grew from 2,176 residents in 1950 to 23,322 in 2020, so most of the town's houses were built in the decades since the war. Homes from the 1950s through the 1970s often have cast iron or clay building drains whose rough, scaled walls catch grease and tree roots far more easily than modern PVC.

That matters for the repair decision. If a line clogs again a few weeks after it was cleared, the pipe wall is usually the cause, and a camera inspection of the sewer line shows whether cleaning is enough or the pipe needs work.

Whether your address sits on town sewer or a septic system also changes the diagnosis, because a septic tank that is full behaves like a clogged main. If you are unsure which you have, tell us and we will check before running a machine.

Kitchen sink clogs

.

Kitchen drains clog differently from bathroom ones. Grease cools inside the pipe and coats the wall, food solids catch on the coating, and a disposal grinds scraps into a paste that settles at the first bend.

If both basins back up together, the blockage is past the point where the two sinks join, and if the dishwasher drains into the same line you may see grey water in the sink after a cycle. A cable machine usually clears this, and a hydro jet scours the grease layer away when the clog keeps coming back.

When it is a leak and not a clog

.

A wet spot at the base of a wall, a stain on a basement ceiling under a bathroom or a floor that stays damp after the drain runs can mean the drain line itself is leaking. Cleaning will not fix a cracked or separated joint, and running water through it makes the leak worse.

In that case the next step is finding the exact spot, which is the work of leak detection, followed by a repair or section replacement.

Common questions

.
Do you handle drain cleaning emergencies at night or on weekends in Bedford?

Yes. The line is answered 24/7, and a plumber is sent as soon as one is free. Tell the dispatcher if water or sewage is coming up so your call is placed ahead of a single slow sink.

Should I try chemical drain cleaner before calling?

We advise against it. The chemicals can sit in a fully blocked pipe, damage older metal fittings and splash on the person who opens the line. Plain hot water and a plunger are the safest first steps.

Why does my drain clog again a few weeks later?

A repeat clog usually means the pipe wall is rough, bellied or partly broken, so material keeps catching in the same spot. A camera inspection shows which, and hydro jetting often resets the line while you plan a repair.

Will cleaning damage an older pipe?

Cleaning with the right head is safe on sound pipe. Fragile cast iron or cracked clay is checked by camera first, so we do not push a cutter through a line that cannot take it.

Is the clog in my house or in the town sewer?

If only your fixtures are affected and a cleanout is clear on the street side, the clog is in your line. Neighbors with the same problem or sewage at the lowest drain in several homes points to the public main, and the town should be told.

Do you clear drains outside Bedford?

Yes. We work in Manchester, Merrimack, Goffstown, Amherst and Hooksett, and the same 24/7 line covers all of them.
