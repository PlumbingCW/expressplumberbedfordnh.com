Emergency Water Heater Repair in Bedford, NH

.

A water heater that is leaking onto the floor or has gone cold is an emergency, and our 24/7 line answers at any hour. While you wait, switch off the power at the breaker or turn the gas control to pilot, close the cold-water valve on top of the tank and keep the area around the unit clear.

Water heater repair means diagnosing why a unit stopped heating, leaks or runs noisily, then replacing the failed part such as an element, thermostat, valve or anode rod. We repair electric, gas and heat pump water heaters in Bedford, NH, and we tell you honestly when a tank is past repairing.

Water heater leaking: what to do first

.

Not every drip means the tank has failed. A leak from a pipe fitting or the relief valve can often be repaired, while water seeping from the tank body itself usually cannot.

  1. Turn off the electricity at the breaker, or set a gas unit's control to pilot or off.
  2. Close the cold-water supply valve above the heater so the tank stops refilling.
  3. Place a bucket under any drip and move anything stored near the unit.
  4. If the leak is large, drain the tank through its hose valve to a floor drain or outdoors.
  5. Call the 24/7 line and tell us the leak location, because that decides what we bring.

Evening and weekend leaks are common because heaters tend to fail while under steady use. We take same-day appointments when a plumber is open and treat an active leak as the priority call.

Where we see failures in Bedford homes

.

Households in Bedford range from older ranches on private wells to newer subdivisions on public water, and the water behind the heater changes what fails. Homes supplied by Manchester Water Works draw from Lake Massabesic, whose published 2018 report lists total hardness at 19.35 mg/L, which is soft water.

Well water is a different story. NHDES recommends that private well owners test every three to five years, and minerals or iron found in that test explain sediment, rotten-egg smells and short element life. If you have not tested, a lab result is the quickest way to know why your heater keeps needing parts.

Electric, gas and heat pump units

.

Electric tank heaters fail mostly at the elements and thermostats, which are inexpensive parts on a healthy tank. Gas units add the burner, the pilot assembly and the venting, and a draft or flue problem is treated as a safety call, never a quick fix.

Hybrid heat pump water heaters sit on top of a tank and add a compressor, a fan and a condensate drain. Their faults are different, so we confirm the model before quoting. Gas-to-heat-pump swaps are a replacement job, covered on our water heater replacement page.

Repair or replace the tank?

.

A rule that holds up is to weigh the unit's age against the repair. A heater past 10 to 12 years with a rusted base or a leak from the tank body is a replacement. A younger unit with a bad element or valve is almost always worth repairing.

Keep an eye on the relief valve, because a valve that drips often signals high pressure or a failing expansion tank, and both are cheap to fix compared with a flooded basement. If the tank is the problem, a planned water heater installation beats an emergency swap at midnight.

Common questions

.
Do you handle water heater emergencies at night or on weekends in Bedford?

Yes. Our dispatch line is open 24/7. A leaking tank is placed ahead of routine calls, and same-day appointments are offered whenever a plumber is free.

Can a leaking water heater be repaired?

If the leak comes from a fitting, the relief valve or a drain valve, yes. If water is seeping through the tank wall, the tank has failed and replacement is the sensible route.

How long do water heaters last?

Tank heaters commonly run around 10 to 15 years depending on water quality and maintenance. A unit near the end of that range with a new problem is often better replaced than repaired.

Why does my hot water smell like sulfur?

A rotten-egg smell usually comes from bacteria reacting with the anode rod in the tank. Flushing the tank and replacing the rod often fixes it, and well owners should also test the source water.

Is a dripping relief valve dangerous?

It is a warning sign. The valve is opening because of heat or pressure, so we test pressure and the expansion tank rather than just swapping the valve.
