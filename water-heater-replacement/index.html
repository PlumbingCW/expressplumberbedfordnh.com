Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Bedford, NH

.

A tank that has split, flooded the floor or died completely is an emergency, and our 24/7 line is answered at any hour. While you wait, shut off power or gas to the heater, close the cold-water valve above it and keep the area clear.

Water heater replacement is the removal of a failed or end-of-life unit and the installation of a new one that fits the home's demand, fuel and venting. In Bedford, NH most replacements are like-for-like tanks, with growing interest in heat pump and tankless models.

When a failed water heater is an emergency

.

A tank leaking from its body cannot be repaired, and the water it releases can damage flooring, drywall and a finished basement within hours. A heater with no hot water in January is also urgent for a household with children or elderly residents.

  1. Switch off the breaker or set the gas control to off.
  2. Close the cold-water supply valve above the tank.
  3. Open a hot tap upstairs to break the vacuum, then drain the tank through its valve if water is still flowing.
  4. Photograph the unit, the label with its age, and any damage.

Replacement the same day is possible when a unit of the right size is available and a plumber is free. If a replacement must be ordered, we can make the leak safe and set a date rather than leave you with a flooded floor.

Sizing the new heater for a Bedford household

.

Tank size follows household demand. A couple with showers at different times does well with a 40-gallon tank, while a family of four with a soaking tub usually needs 50 to 80 gallons, or a unit with a faster recovery rate.

Many Bedford houses are larger than the 1950s ranches the town started with, and big master baths put heavy demand on a single tank. We ask how many bathrooms, which fixtures run together and how large the largest tub is before recommending a size.

Tank, heat pump or tankless

.

The decision usually comes down to fuel, space and budget.

Option.Strength.Trade-off.
Standard tank (electric or gas).Lowest upfront cost and easy swap.Standby heat loss and a limited hot-water supply.
Heat pump (hybrid) tank.Uses a fraction of the electricity of a standard electric tank.Needs space, clearance and a condensate drain.
Tankless.Endless hot water and a small footprint.Higher cost, venting and gas line upgrades, and yearly flushing.

Switching a gas heater to a heat pump unit is a real option if you want to move off gas. It changes the venting, electrical and drain details, so we walk the house before quoting. For planned changes, see water heater installation and tankless service.

What happens on replacement day

.

We confirm the old unit's fuel, size and venting, shut off supply, drain the tank and disconnect it. The new heater is set, connected with fresh flex connectors and shutoff valves and fitted with a new relief valve and discharge pipe.

Gas units get a combustion and venting check. Electric units get the correct breaker and wire size confirmed. We then fill the tank, purge air, test for leaks and show you the shutoff locations.

Permit and inspection rules vary by town and fuel type, so ask the Bedford town office whether your replacement needs one. We will tell you what we find in the house that could affect it.

No hot water in the middle of winter

.

A heater that fails in a cold snap is an easy day to underestimate. If a tank is mid-leak, close the supply and call even if it is two in the morning, because the cold-water valve is the only thing standing between a drip and a flood.

If the cause is a gas-supply fault instead of the tank, we check for gas odor first. If you smell gas, leave the home and call the gas utility or 911 before you call a plumber.

What affects replacement cost

.

The final price depends on the following, and we put the number in writing before starting.

Fuel and type.Heat pump and tankless units cost more upfront than a standard tank.
Tank size.A bigger tank costs more and weighs more to move.
Venting and gas or electrical work.Updating a flue, adding a drain or running a new circuit changes the total.
Access and removal.Stairs, tight closets and old-tank haul-away take time.
Time of the call.Emergency after-hours replacement carries a different rate from a scheduled day.

Insurance covers sudden water damage from a failed heater in many policies, but not the heater itself. Keep the old unit's label and your photographs for the claim.

Call with the make and age of the unit, and a dispatcher will price the swap before work starts.

Common questions

.
Do you handle water heater replacement emergencies at night or on weekends in Bedford?

Yes. The line is staffed 24/7. We make a leaking tank safe first, and same-day replacement is offered when a plumber and a suitable unit are available.

How long does a replacement take?

A straight tank swap commonly takes a few hours. Heat pump, tankless or venting changes take longer and sometimes need a second visit.

Can I switch from gas to a heat pump water heater?

Yes, but it changes venting, power and drainage requirements. We assess the space and electrical panel before quoting.

Does the old heater get hauled away?

Yes. We disconnect, remove and dispose of the old unit as part of the job.

What size tank do I need?

It depends on bathrooms, simultaneous use and your largest tub. A short conversation with the dispatcher or technician settles it.

Do you replace heaters in other towns?

Yes. We serve Manchester, Merrimack, Goffstown, Amherst and Hooksett from the same dispatch line.

Standards and references

.
