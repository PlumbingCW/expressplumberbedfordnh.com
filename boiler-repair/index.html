Emergency Boiler Repair in Bedford, NH

.

A boiler that stops in a New Hampshire winter leaves a house cold within hours, and a boiler that leaks adds water damage to the problem. Our 24/7 line answers at any hour, and while you wait you should close the water feed to the boiler, keep baseboards clear and use safe supplemental heat.

Boiler repair on our side covers the water side of a hydronic system, including leaks, circulator pumps, zone valves, expansion tanks, relief valves and air in the lines. Burner, combustion and flue work is outside our scope, and we will tell you when to call a licensed heating technician.

No heat or a leak: what is an emergency

.

A cold house in January is urgent. So is any boiler that is leaking or has a relief valve that discharges.

  • No heat and the house is cooling toward freezing.
  • Water leaking from the boiler, piping or relief valve.
  • Banging, knocking or gurgling in the pipes.
  • A pressure gauge reading very high or at zero.

Tell the dispatcher what you see. We offer same-day visits when a plumber is free and answer at night and on weekends.

  1. If you smell gas or see soot, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 first.
  2. If water is leaking, close the cold-water feed valve to the boiler.
  3. Do not touch the relief valve or the burner.
  4. Open interior doors and use safe space heating while you wait.

Common water-side problems

.

Most hydronic problems come down to a few causes.

  • A failed circulator pump means the boiler is hot but the house is cold.
  • A stuck zone valve leaves one area of the house without heat.
  • A waterlogged expansion tank causes the relief valve to open.
  • Air trapped in the loop produces banging and cold radiators at the top of the house.
  • A leak from old fittings drops pressure and forces constant refilling.

Constant refilling adds fresh oxygen and minerals to the loop, which speeds corrosion. If your boiler needs water often, call us to find the leak.

Why winter calls come in clusters

.

Bedford homes built in the decades after 1950 often have the original hot water heating system or a replacement in an old layout. Systems that have run quietly in autumn get their first hard test when the temperature drops into single digits.

A service visit before the heating season catches worn circulators and leaking valves before they fail. If you cannot do that, know where your boiler's cold-water feed, power switch and zone valves are, so you can act quickly.

What affects the cost of boiler repair

.

The final price depends on these factors.

The failed part.A pump or zone valve costs more than a small fitting.
Age of the system.Old piping and fittings may need more than the one failed part.
Access.Tight mechanical rooms and finished ceilings slow the work.
Draining and refilling the loop.Large systems take time to drain, repair and refill.
Time of the call.A winter night visit is priced differently from a scheduled one.

Homeowners insurance usually covers water damage from a sudden leak, but not wear and tear on the boiler. Photograph the damage before you clean.

Common questions

.
Do you handle boiler emergencies at night or on weekends in Bedford?

Yes. The line is open 24/7. A leak or a loss of heat in winter is placed ahead of routine calls, and same-day service is offered when a plumber is free.

Do you repair burners and furnaces?

No. We repair the water side of hydronic boilers. For burner, combustion or flue problems, we recommend a licensed heating technician.

Why does my boiler keep losing pressure?

A leak in the loop or a failed expansion tank is the usual cause. We find the leak and test the tank.

Why is my radiator cold at the top?

Trapped air. Bleeding the radiator or purging the zone usually fixes it, and a persistent problem points to an air eliminator.

What should I do if I smell gas?

Leave the house and call the gas utility or 911. Do not operate switches or phones inside.
