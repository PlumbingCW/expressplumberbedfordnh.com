Basement Waterproofing in Bedford, NH

.

A basement that leaks every spring, smells musty or shows white mineral lines on the wall has a water problem that more paint will not solve. Our dispatch line is open 24/7, and an active flood is handled first through our sump pump service.

Basement waterproofing is the planned work of directing water away from the foundation and out of the basement, using exterior grading, interior drainage, a sump pump and sealing. In Bedford, NH, where most homes sit on full basements built after 1950, it is among the most useful investments in a house.

Where basement water comes from

.

Water does not enter by accident. It follows a path, and the first job is finding it.

Source.Sign.Typical remedy.
Surface runoff.Water at the wall after rain, near downspouts.Regrade, extend downspouts, clean gutters.
Rising groundwater.Water at the floor and wall joint, rising with spring melt.Interior drainage and a sump pump.
Wall cracks.Water along a vertical or stair-step crack.Crack injection or exterior repair.
Window wells.Water below windows.Cover, drain or replace the well.
Condensation.Damp on walls in summer.Dehumidify, insulate cold pipes.
Plumbing leak.Damp spot not tied to weather.Check supply and drain lines.

A leaking pipe can look like a foundation problem. If water appears in dry weather, a leak detection visit rules plumbing in or out.

When it is an emergency and when it is planning

.

If water is on the floor right now, the job is to get it out. Our sump pump service covers failed pumps and active flooding, and the 24/7 line answers at night.

Waterproofing planning comes after the water is under control. It is a dry-day job that deals with the cause, so the next storm or thaw does not repeat the damage.

Waterproofing methods compared

.

There is no single fix. The right plan combines methods based on what the inspection finds.

Method.How it works.Good for.
Exterior grading and gutters.Directs roof and surface water away.Water that arrives from above the foundation.
Interior perimeter drain.Collects water at the footing and sends it to a sump.Rising groundwater and wall seepage.
Sump pump and pit.Removes collected water from the basement.Any system with a drain.
Crack injection.Seals specific wall cracks.Isolated cracks that leak.
Sealants and coatings.Reduce dampness on the wall.Minor moisture, never major flow.

What we do and what we leave to others

.

As a plumbing company, we handle the parts of a waterproofing plan that involve pipes, pumps and drainage. That means interior drains, sump pits and pumps, discharge lines, floor drain work and backwater valves.

Large exterior excavations, foundation structural repairs and major regrading are often better done by a foundation or excavation contractor, and we will tell you when a job falls outside our scope. We coordinate where it matters, so the drain we install matches the exterior work.

Bedford homes and the spring thaw

.

Bedford grew rapidly after 1950, and many homes from that era have poured-concrete or block foundations that are now several decades old. Footing drains may be clogged, clay tile may have collapsed and original waterproofing coats have thinned.

Spring is when these weaknesses show. Snowmelt and rain on frozen ground sit against the foundation, and any gap becomes a leak. Look at your basement in March and April, and note where water appears so we can plan around it.

What affects the cost of basement waterproofing

.

The price depends on the size and condition of the basement.

Length of drain.A single wall costs less than the full perimeter.
Water volume.A heavy flow needs a larger pump and a backup.
Floor and finish.Finished basements require careful access and restoration.
Discharge route.Where the pump sends water matters, and a long run adds cost.

Most homeowners policies exclude groundwater seepage. A water backup endorsement may help with sump failures, but it does not pay for waterproofing itself. Ask your agent.

Common questions

.
Do you handle basement water emergencies at night or on weekends in Bedford?

Yes. The line is staffed 24/7 for flooding. Planned waterproofing is scheduled for a dry day, but we place active water ahead of everything.

Will interior drainage fix my wet basement?

In most cases it manages the water that reaches the footing, but it works best with good gutters and grading outside.

Is a dehumidifier enough?

It treats humidity, not water entering the basement. If you see standing water or seepage, you need drainage.

How do I know whether it is a leak or condensation?

Tape a square of plastic wrap to the wall for a day. Moisture on the room side means condensation, and on the wall side means seepage.

Do I need a permit?

It depends on the work and the town. Ask the Bedford building department about drainage and electrical changes.
