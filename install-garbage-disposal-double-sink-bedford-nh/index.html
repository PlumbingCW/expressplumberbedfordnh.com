Installing a Garbage Disposal in a Double Sink: Bedford, NH Homeowner Guide

.

A disposal is one of the more satisfying kitchen upgrades, and a double sink adds a wrinkle. The drain layout changes, and the second basin needs a way to connect without a leak.

This guide covers the steps and the traps to avoid.

Call (603) 815-9179.

By the Bedford Express Plumbing team · Published 2026-11-10

How a double sink drain changes

.

In a double sink, the disposal usually sits under one basin, and the other basin drains into it or to a nearby trap. A continuous waste tee or a branch tailpiece carries the second basin's water.

That tee must be placed so water flows down toward the trap, with no low spots. The disposal outlet must connect to the trap at the right height, and the layout depends on the model.

Parts and tools

.

Gather everything before you cut a pipe.

  • The disposal with its mounting kit.
  • Plumber's putty or a gasket for the sink flange.
  • A discharge tube or elbow and slip nuts.
  • A power cord or wiring kit, if the disposal does not come with a cord.
  • Hose clamps for the dishwasher connection.
  • Pliers, a screwdriver and a bucket.

Mounting the disposal

.

Remove the old strainer basket and clean the opening. Press putty around the new flange and seat it from above, then tighten the mounting ring underneath until the flange sits firmly.

  1. Attach the mounting assembly and set the disposal on the lower ring.
  2. Twist the disposal until the discharge outlet lines up with the drain pipe.
  3. Lock it in place with the mounting ring.
  4. Knock out the dishwasher port if you are connecting a dishwasher.
  5. Connect the discharge elbow and the trap arm.

Dishwasher and the second basin

.

A dishwasher drain hose connects to a small inlet on the side of the disposal. The plug inside that inlet must be knocked out with a hammer and screwdriver, or the dishwasher will not drain.

The second basin connects to the disposal's outlet tee or to a branch above it. Test by filling both basins and letting them drain together, watching every joint.

Wiring safely

.

Most disposals plug into an outlet under the sink, controlled by a wall switch. If there is no outlet, one must be installed by a licensed electrician or a plumber qualified to do so.

Turn off the breaker before you touch any wire, and keep connections dry and secured. A tripped breaker or humming unit after installation points to a jam or a wiring problem.

When to call a plumber

.

Testing and troubleshooting after installation

.

Run cold water, switch the disposal on and let it spin for ten seconds before you grind anything. Fill both basins and release them together, watching every connection under the sink for a drip.

Problem.Likely cause.Fix.
Leak at the top flange.Putty or gasket not seated.Remove, reseat with fresh putty.
Leak at the side.Loose discharge connection.Tighten the slip nut or replace the washer.
Dishwasher will not drain.Knockout plug left in.Remove the plug from inside the inlet.
Disposal hums but will not turn.Jam.Free it with the hex wrench at the bottom, after power is off.

Tighten everything again after a day of use. New washers settle, and a small drip that appears overnight is easier to fix than one found in a month.

Bedford note: sewer or septic

.

A garbage disposal sends extra solids into your drain system, and where that goes depends on whether your house is on public sewer or a septic system.

Ask the Bedford town office which applies to your address. Septic owners should ask their septic service whether a disposal suits their tank, since ground food adds to the solids that settle.

Common questions

.
Can I install a disposal in a double sink myself?

If there is an outlet and the drain layout fits, yes. If you need new wiring or a new drain layout, call a professional.

Do I need to knock out the dishwasher plug?

Yes, if the dishwasher connects to the disposal. If you forget, the dishwasher will not drain.

How long does an install take?

A swap takes an hour or two. A first installation with new wiring takes longer.

Is a disposal bad for septic systems?

It adds solids to the tank, so septic owners should ask their septic service before installing one.

Is my Bedford home on sewer or septic?

Ask the Bedford town office. The answer changes whether a disposal is a good idea.

Standards and references

.
