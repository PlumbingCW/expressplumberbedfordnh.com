Where South River Road is

.

South River Road is a long corridor on the southern side of Bedford. It runs from the Manchester side of town toward Merrimack, and it passes through very different kinds of property.

We do not draw a boundary for the corridor on this page. If you are on or just off South River Road, call and the dispatcher will confirm your address.

Homes and businesses are different jobs

.

A condominium, a multifamily building and a retail space each have different plumbing and different people responsible for it. We keep these separate so the right plumber and the right price go to the right call.

For offices, shops and multi-unit buildings that are run as businesses, see commercial plumbing. The rest of this page is for residents.

Condo or multifamily: who is responsible?

.

In a condominium or multi-unit building, responsibility for a pipe depends on your governing documents. A pipe inside your unit is often the owner's, while a shared line or riser is often the association's.

We do not know your documents, so check them or ask your property manager before authorizing work. Tell the dispatcher if the problem may be on a shared line, and we will explain what we find.

Calls common in shared buildings

.

Shared buildings bring their own problems, and the lowest unit usually feels a main-line clog first. These are the services that apply most often.

  • Sewer line cleaning when several fixtures back up at once.
  • Drain cleaning for a single slow tub, sink or kitchen line.
  • Water heater replacement for a failed unit inside your space.
  • Leak detection when a stain appears and the source is unclear.

Describe the problem to the dispatcher and you will get a clear price before work starts.

Water and utilities: ask the town

.

Water and sewer service vary by address, and we do not assume what applies to your unit. Your property manager or the Bedford town offices can tell you.

For a larger job, ask the town what permit applies before work starts.

Common questions

.
Is South River Road a residential or commercial area?

It is both. Bedford's master plan calls it the town's largest concentration of commercial development, and it also includes multifamily, condominium and mixed-use settings.

Who pays for a clog in a shared line?

It depends on where the blockage is and on your governing documents. Check them or ask your property manager, and tell the dispatcher it may be a shared line.

Do you take business plumbing calls on this road?

Yes, through commercial plumbing. Tell the dispatcher it is a business and we will route the call accordingly.

Can I get a same-day appointment?

Same-day appointments are offered when a plumber is available. Call early and describe the problem.

Standards and references

.

Plumbing work follows published model codes such as the International Plumbing Code (IPC) from the International Code Council and the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) from IAPMO. The edition a town enforces is its own decision, so ask the Bedford building department which one applies to your project.
