Emergency Toilet Repair in Bedford, NH

.

A toilet that is overflowing or leaking at the base is an emergency, and our 24/7 line answers at any hour. While you wait, turn the shutoff valve behind the toilet clockwise, lift the tank lid and set the float up to stop the fill, and mop standing water so it does not reach the subfloor.

Toilet repair and installation covers clogs, running tanks, leaking bases, failed flush parts and replacement with a new bowl. In Bedford, NH most calls come down to a clog, a worn flapper or fill valve, or a wax ring that has failed.

When a toilet problem is an emergency

.

Most toilet problems can wait for the next opening. A few cannot.

  • Water is overflowing the bowl or rising toward the rim.
  • Water leaks from the base of the toilet onto the floor.
  • The toilet is the only one in the house and will not flush.
  • Several drains gurgle when the toilet is flushed, which can mean a main line clog.

If it is overflowing, tell the dispatcher in your first sentence. We offer same-day visits when a plumber is free and answer at night and on weekends.

  1. Do not flush again.
  2. Reach behind the toilet and turn the shutoff valve clockwise.
  3. Lift the tank lid and raise the float to stop water from entering.
  4. Use towels to contain water and lay a bucket under any leak.
  5. If the water is gray or smells like sewage, keep everyone away and call.

A toilet that clogs again and again

.

A single clog is usually something that was flushed. A toilet that clogs again and again suggests another cause, such as a partly blocked trap, a vent problem, a low-flow bowl that cannot move a load or a main line that is slowly closing.

If the other fixtures in the house are also slow, the problem is not the toilet, and sewer line cleaning is the next step. If only this bowl is affected, an auger and a close look at the flange often settle it.

Installing or replacing a toilet

.

A new toilet is a straightforward upgrade when the old one is cracked, stained inside or wasteful. We remove the old unit, inspect the flange and subfloor for rot, set a new wax ring or seal, level the bowl, connect the supply and test the flush.

Choices include round or elongated bowls, one-piece or two-piece designs, comfort-height seats and water-saving models. Bedford homes that were remodeled in different decades have a mix of rough-in sizes, so we measure the distance from the wall to the flange before you buy.

If you are remodeling a bathroom or adding one, new fixtures and lines are covered in plumbing installation.

Common questions

.
Do you handle toilet emergencies at night or on weekends in Bedford?

Yes. The line is answered 24/7, and an overflowing or leaking toilet is placed ahead of routine calls. Same-day service is offered when a plumber is free.

Can I use a plunger on an overflowing toilet?

Close the shutoff and raise the float first, then use a flange plunger gently. If the water rises again, stop and call.

Why does my toilet run for a few seconds on its own?

It is usually a flapper that no longer seals. The tank slowly leaks into the bowl and refills. It is an inexpensive fix that also lowers your water bill.

How long does it take to replace a toilet?

A straight swap is usually done within a couple of hours, unless the flange or floor needs repair.

Is a wobbly toilet a problem?

Yes. A rocking toilet breaks the wax seal, which can leak into the subfloor for months before anyone notices. Have it reset.
