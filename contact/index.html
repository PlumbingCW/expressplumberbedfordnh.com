What to tell the dispatcher

.

A few details help us send the right plumber with the right equipment.

  1. Your address and the nearest cross street.
  2. What is happening, such as a leak, a backup or no hot water.
  3. Whether water or sewage is spreading, and where the main shutoff is.
  4. Any equipment details, such as the make and age of a water heater.
