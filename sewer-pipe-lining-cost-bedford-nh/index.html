Sewer Pipe Lining Cost in Bedford, NH: Price Guide

.

Pipe lining repairs a damaged sewer line from the inside, so you do not have to dig a trench across the yard. It is not cheap, and it is not right for every pipe, but for the right job it saves a driveway, a patio or a mature tree.

This guide explains how lining works, what drives the price and how to decide between lining and digging. We do not list fixed dollar figures, because the number depends on your line.

What pipe lining is

.

Lining, also called cured-in-place pipe, starts with a camera. A flexible resin-soaked liner is pulled or inverted into the old pipe, inflated against the walls and cured with hot water, steam or light. When it hardens, it forms a new pipe inside the old one.

The liner seals cracks and joints and stops roots from entering. It does not fix a pipe that has collapsed or a belly that holds water, so the camera comes first.

What drives the price

.

Prices vary because every line is different. These are the factors that move the number.

Length of the line.The longer the run, the more liner and time.
Pipe diameter and condition.Larger pipes and heavily damaged pipes cost more to prepare.
Cleaning and prep.Roots and scale must be removed first, often with a jet.
Access.A cleanout makes setup easier. Missing cleanouts add work.
Number of connections and bends.Lateral connections must be reopened after lining.
Timing.An emergency job is priced differently from a planned one.

Ask for a written quote that lists the camera work, the cleaning, the liner and the reopening of connections, so you can compare bids honestly.

Lining or digging: which fits

.

Neither method is always better. The right one depends on the pipe and what sits above it.

Question.Lining.Digging and replacing.
Disturbance to the yard.Small access pits.A full trench.
Works on collapsed pipe.No.Yes.
Works on a belly.No.Yes, with new slope.
Best for.Cracks, root intrusion and joint leaks.Collapsed, offset or sagging pipe.
Under a driveway or patio.Often the practical choice.Requires restoration.
Pipe life.Manufacturers cite decades of service.A new pipe lasts for decades.

If your camera shows damage that lining cannot fix, the answer is sewer line repair by excavation.

Why a camera inspection comes first

.

No one can responsibly price a lining job without seeing the pipe. A sewer camera inspection shows the length, the material, the condition and any low spots, and it gives you a recording to share with other contractors.

If a contractor quotes lining sight unseen, ask why. A good quote starts with footage.

Warning signs your sewer line needs attention

.

Most homeowners learn about a failing sewer line from a symptom.

  • Drains that clog again within weeks of being cleared.
  • Gurgling in toilets when a sink or washer drains.
  • A sewage smell near the cleanout or basement.
  • Wet or sunken patches in the yard over the line.
  • Roots visible in a camera view or in what comes out of the line.

If sewage is already backing up, that is an emergency, and our sewer line cleaning team can open the line first.

Questions to ask any lining contractor

.

Lining is a specialized trade, and quality varies. A short list of questions separates careful contractors from the rest.

  1. Will you camera the line before and after, and give me the footage?
  2. How will you clean the pipe, and what will you do about roots?
  3. How will you reopen the side connections after the liner cures?
  4. What happens at the ends, where the liner meets the old pipe?
  5. What does your warranty cover, and for how long?

Ask for the liner's material and thickness in writing. A thin liner on a badly damaged pipe fails sooner, and a contractor who cannot explain the difference deserves another question or two.

Insurance, warranties and getting quotes

.

Homeowners insurance usually does not pay for a worn-out sewer line, though some policies offer a service line endorsement. Check before work starts, because coverage often requires approval in advance.

Get at least two written quotes that include camera footage, ask about the warranty on the liner and confirm who handles permits. Ask the Bedford building department whether a permit applies to your job.

Common questions

.
Does lining work on every pipe?

No. It works on pipe that is still round and structurally sound enough to hold the liner. Collapsed or badly offset pipe needs repair or replacement.

How long does lining last?

Manufacturers describe service lives of several decades. The result depends on the prep and on the condition of the host pipe.

How long does a lining job take?

Many are done in a day, plus time for cleaning and cure. Longer runs and complex connections take more.

Can lining stop tree roots?

It seals the joints and cracks roots use to enter, so it stops most root intrusion. Roots outside the pipe may keep growing but they can no longer get in.

How do I know if my Bedford street is on town sewer?

Ask the Bedford town office, and look for a cleanout and a municipal sewer connection in your records. Homes on septic follow different rules.

Standards and references

.

Plumbing work follows published model codes such as the International Plumbing Code (IPC) from the International Code Council and the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) from IAPMO. The edition a town enforces is its own decision, so ask the Bedford building department which one applies to your project.
